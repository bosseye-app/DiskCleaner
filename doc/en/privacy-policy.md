# DiskCleaner — Privacy policy

2026-11-27

This policy explains how DiskCleaner handles information on your Mac.

In short: the app only reads the folders you authorize, and no file content or path ever leaves your Mac.

No account is required. The app does not collect your name, email address or phone number.

There is no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting code, and no device fingerprint is created.

The app runs no server of its own and uploads nothing. The only network activity is the App Store purchase, handled by Apple.

While scanning, the app reads file names, paths, sizes and modification dates. They are used on this Mac only, to measure and display results.

Scan results, deletion history, language and appearance settings are stored locally and can be cleared inside the app.

Folder access is granted through the system folder picker and stored as a local security bookmark. You can revoke it at any time in System Settings.

Deleting moves items to the macOS Trash. The app keeps no copy, and it cannot restore anything once the Trash is emptied.

Purchases are processed by Apple. The app only learns whether the full version is unlocked; it never sees payment details.

No third-party SDK is bundled, and your files, paths and history are never sold or shared.

The app is not directed at children and collects nothing from them. If this policy changes, the date below changes with it. Questions are welcome through the support page.
